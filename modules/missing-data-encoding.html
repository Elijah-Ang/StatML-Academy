01The problem

What might an empty cell mean?

An empty cell does not automatically mean zero. It may mean not measured, not available, not applicable or withheld. Find out which meaning applies before changing it.

Why can mean filling distort an analysis?

Filling every blank with the mean adds central values. This can make the data look less spread out. If large values are more often missing, the observed mean can also be biased.

Why this works · a little deeper

If high earners are more likely to leave income blank, filling with the observed mean pulls those rows towards the middle. It can distort means and spread. A missingness indicator records whether the value was absent; it may help prediction, but does not prove a missing-at-random assumption for statistical inference.

04Reasoning with the method

How do MCAR, MAR, MNAR, and encoding differ?

MCAR, MAR and MNAR describe assumptions about why values are missing. One-hot and ordinal encoding describe how we represent categories. A picture of blanks cannot prove the reason for missingness.

A small example · separate from the live dataset
Follow the ideaRead it in plain language
MCARMissingness unrelated to observed or missing values
MARExplainable by observed values
MNARDepends on unseen values after conditioning

Try it

Switch MCAR, MAR and MNAR. Compare which group or values tend to become hollow crossed marks. Their true positions are visible only because this is a simulation.

Why this works · a little deeper

MCAR means missingness does not depend on the relevant observed or missing values. MAR means, after conditioning on observed information, it does not depend on the missing value. MNAR means dependence on the unobserved value remains. These are assumptions, not diagnoses proved by a chart. One-hot makes separate yes/no columns for unordered categories. Ordinal encoding asserts an order, so use it only when that order is justified.

Quick check · Can visible blanks alone prove that missing values caused their own absence?

No. The missingness mechanism needs assumptions and outside knowledge; the blanks alone cannot identify MNAR.

06Assumptions and failure modes

Switch one-hot and ordinal coding. Read Red, Blue, Green, and New? headings. Compare Purple’s unknown handling with the distances implied by ordinal codes.

Why this works · a little deeper

Assumptions

The analysis relies on stated assumptions about why values are missing. Category representation must match meaningful order or lack of order. Learn data-based transforms only within training.

When the method fails

Single imputation treats one filled value as if known, often understating inference uncertainty. Multiple imputation combines several plausible filled datasets, accounting for within- and between-imputation variation under its assumptions. Target encoding summarizes outcomes by category and needs fold-safe or cross-fitted training representations. Plain numeric category codes can create unjustified distances.

Quick check · Can target encoding use a row's own outcome for its training representation without caution?

That can create leakage; use appropriate cross-fitting.

07Practical workflow

What belongs in the fitted pipeline?

Split first. Learn the filling rule and category mapping on training rows. Save what to do with unknown categories. Apply those same rules to new rows without silently relearning them.

Profile blanks and category levels, distinguish structural absence from accidental missingness, split appropriately, and learn transforms inside training. New categories should follow the saved policy rather than cause an unexplained failure or arbitrary numeric label.

Target encoding should be…

Answer and explanation

Cross-fitted inside training data. Each held-out row must not influence its own encoded target mean.

Single imputation for inference often…

Answer and explanation

Understates uncertainty. Treating filled values as known ignores imputation uncertainty.

Integer codes for unordered colours create…

Answer and explanation

Artificial order and distance. Codes such as red=1, blue=2, green=3 imply geometry that may not exist.

An unseen category policy belongs…

Answer and explanation

In the fitted pipeline. Deployment must define how new levels are represented.

A missingness indicator…

Answer and explanation

May help prediction but does not prove a missingness mechanism. It is a feature, not a proof about why data are absent.
