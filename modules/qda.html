01The Raw Data Cloud

What differs between these class clouds?

QDA models each class with its own spread shape. One class can be broad and tilted while another is tight and round. The model compares how well a new point fits each class's probability cloud.

Why this works · a little deeper

QDA is a supervised classifier: fitting uses known class labels. Its usual model gives each class a multivariate Gaussian, or bell-shaped cloud in several inputs. Equal-density contours describe the model, not physical fruit boundaries. The highest posterior, meaning class probability after seeing the inputs, supplies the usual label decision.

Quick check · Does QDA only differ from LDA in its class means?

No. Both have class means; QDA allows different covariance shapes.

03The Straight-Line Trap (LDA)

When is a straight boundary too restrictive?

LDA forces the classes to share a spread shape. QDA allows different shapes. That can change where their scores tie, creating a curved rather than straight decision boundary.

Why this works · a little deeper

LDA pools within-class spread, rather than forcing the full raw dataset into one grand-centred covariance. QDA compares separate class shapes. More flexibility is not automatically better: a curved fit to noise may predict worse than a straight rule. Compare on rows outside fitting.

04Fitting QDA: What the Model Learns

Which parameters does QDA estimate?

For each class, QDA estimates a centre, a covariance shape and a prior. The prior is the class's starting frequency. A separate shape uses many estimates, so small classes can make fitting unstable.

Why this works · a little deeper

For two features, covariance is a 2×2 table. The diagonal gives each feature's variance; the off-diagonal gives their linear co-movement. μ is the centre and Σ the covariance. The determinant |Σ| measures covariance volume scaling; √|Σ| relates to geometric area or volume. Σ⁻¹ adjusts distances for shape. Too few independent rows or redundant inputs can make the inverse unavailable or unstable.

Quick check · Does adding dimensions increase covariance parameters only linearly?

No. A d-dimensional covariance has d(d + 1)/2 unique entries.

05Testing with Shape-Aware Distance

What is shape-aware distance?

Mahalanobis distance measures a gap relative to the cloud's spread and tilt. A gap along a wide direction is less unusual than the same gap along a narrow one. QDA also considers cloud volume and the prior.

Why this works · a little deeper

Straight-line Euclidean distance does not itself assume classes are circles. It just ignores class-specific spread and tilt. QDA's score also includes −½log|Σ_k| and log prior, not only distance. A broad cloud spreads its density over more space. Model probabilities rely on the fitted distributional assumptions and may be poorly calibrated if those assumptions miss important structure.

Quick check · May the determinant term be dropped when covariance shapes differ?

No. It adjusts the Gaussian density normalization.

06The Curved Boundary

Why do unequal covariance shapes yield a quadratic boundary?

The boundary is where the two class scores are equal. Different covariance shapes can leave squared input terms in that comparison. Those terms allow a quadratic, often curved boundary.

Why this works · a little deeper

The score is −½log|Σ_k| −½(x−μ_k)ᵀΣ_k⁻¹(x−μ_k)+log(π_k), apart from terms shared by classes. At the boundary the scores tie. Shared covariance cancels the quadratic part, giving LDA. Different covariances can retain it. Special cases may still have straight or disconnected boundaries, so do not assume every QDA boundary is one smooth enclosing curve.

Quick check · Why does LDA usually have a simpler boundary?

Its shared covariance cancels the quadratic difference.

08Reading QDA Output

What do prior and covariance outputs mean?

A prior describes how common a class is before seeing this point's inputs. Covariance describes how inputs spread within a class. These are fitted summaries, not claims about what causes the class.

Why this works · a little deeper

A prior of .70 assigns the class a 70% baseline before using the new row's measurements. It is not the fraction the model must predict as that class. Combine the prior with class likelihoods to obtain posterior probabilities. Priors estimated in one population may not suit a later population with different class frequencies.

09Regularization & Shape Playground

What does regularization stabilize?

Shrinkage pulls an unstable covariance estimate towards a simpler target. In this lab, both classes move towards the same pooled shape. At full shrinkage, their shapes match LDA. A small diagonal adjustment has a different purpose.

Why this works · a little deeper

Pooled shrinkage mixes each class covariance with the same pooled covariance. That creates a path towards shared shapes and LDA in this lab. Adding a small identity-matrix term mainly helps make the inverse usable; it does not usually make unequal class shapes equal. Name the target and select the amount using validation. Different software may implement a different target.

Why this works · a little deeper

For K classes and p features, separate symmetric covariances use K×p(p+1)/2 entries. LDA uses one set of p(p+1)/2 instead. Both also estimate class means and priors. For two classes and two features, the usual free-parameter totals are 10 for QDA and 7 for LDA. More parameters can require more representative data. Compare useful performance, calibration, stability and resource cost under the same evaluation plan.

Quick check · Is a tiny training class a reason for caution?
