How do MCAR, MAR, MNAR, and encoding differ?

MCAR, MAR, and MNAR describe assumptions about missingness, while one-hot and ordinal encoding describe representation. A chart of blanks cannot prove the missingness mechanism.

Additional reference · worked numbers below are separate examples from the live notebook.

MCAR, MAR and MNAR are assumptions about the missingness process, not labels that a plot can prove. Complete-case analysis is valid for limited targets under restrictive conditions. Single imputation treats filled values as known and usually understates inferential uncertainty; multiple imputation combines within- and between-imputation variation. Encoding defines geometry: one-hot separates unordered levels, ordinal encoding asserts order, and target encoding borrows outcome information. Target and frequency encoders need fold-safe fitting and an unseen-level policy. Raw columns and a data dictionary should be retained so every transformation remains auditable.

Assumptions

Missing-data methods rely on assumptions about why values are absent. Category encodings assume a structure—unordered, ordinal, or target-related—that must be defensible.

When the method fails

Single imputation understates inferential uncertainty; ordinal codes create fake distances; target encoding leaks without cross-fitting; unseen categories can break deployment.

Can I choose a safe transformation?

Choose both an assumption and a safe pipeline position. A method name alone does not explain whether the transformation is appropriate.

A small example · separate from the live dataset
Follow the ideaRead it in plain language
Unordered categoryUse a representation without invented order
Missing valueState imputation assumptions
EvaluationFit inside training folds
Target encoding should be…

Answer and explanation

Cross-fitted inside training data. Each held-out row must not influence its own encoded target mean.

Single imputation for inference often…

Answer and explanation

Understates uncertainty. Treating filled values as known ignores imputation uncertainty.

Integer codes for unordered colours create…

Answer and explanation

Artificial order and distance. Codes such as red=1, blue=2, green=3 imply geometry that may not exist.

An unseen category policy belongs…

Answer and explanation

In the fitted pipeline. Deployment must define how new levels are represented.

A missingness indicator…

Answer and explanation

May help prediction but does not prove a missingness mechanism. It is a feature, not a proof about why data are absent.
