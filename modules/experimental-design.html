Why are twenty schools not six hundred independent assignments?

If schools are randomized, the school is the assignment unit. Students within a school share treatment and often related outcomes, so 600 rows do not imply 600 independent assignments.

04Reasoning with the method

How do blocking, blinding, and intention-to-treat differ?

Blocking improves balance on known factors; blinding protects measurement or behavior; intention-to-treat analyzes the assigned strategy. None fixes every design problem.

Additional reference · worked numbers below are separate examples from the live notebook.

An estimand specifies population, treatment strategies, comparator, outcome, time and summary contrast. Allocation concealment protects enrolment; blinding protects behaviour and measurement. Blocking randomises within prognostic strata. Cluster randomisation assigns groups and reduces effective sample size through intraclass correlation, so power and analysis must use the randomisation unit. Intention-to-treat preserves the randomised contrast; per-protocol analysis answers a different, more selection-sensitive question. Noncompliance, attrition and interference should be anticipated in the protocol. Power planning must include meaningful effect, variance, allocation, clustering, multiplicity and attrition.

05Interactive laboratory

How does clustering affect effective information?

For equal cluster sizes, design effect is approximately 1 + (m − 1)ICC. Dividing n by this factor gives an illustrative effective sample size, not a complete power calculation.

Additional reference · worked numbers below are separate examples from the live notebook.

For equal cluster sizes m and intraclass correlation ρ, the approximation DE = 1 + (m − 1)ρ describes inflation in variance relative to simple independent sampling under its assumptions. Effective n = n/DE is a rough information comparison. It is not a complete power calculation: power also depends on the number of clusters, allocation, effect and outcome variance, analysis model, unequal sizes, attrition, and multiplicity. The live example reports design effect and approximate effective sample size only.

Assumptions

Assignment is implemented as planned, outcomes are measured consistently, interference is limited or modelled, and attrition does not destroy exchangeability.

When the method fails

Noncompliance, differential attrition, spillovers, outcome switching, hidden multiplicity and analysing below the randomisation unit can bias results or uncertainty.
